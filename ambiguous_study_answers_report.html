Generated from vocab.csv × vocab_study_questions.csv by scripts/find_ambiguous_study_answers.py. A flag means the candidate word's primary English gloss overlaps the question's bolded translation cue and its kana/kanji aren't present in that question's accepted answers — a heuristic over glosses, so borderline cases are worth a human read. Each flag is then checked against the sentence’s grammatical slot using the JMdict part of speech in vocab.csv (a blank before する needs a suru‑verb, before な+noun a na‑adjective, after を a transitive verb, …); candidates that can’t fill that slot are hidden by default and shown under “Grammar conflict” with the reason. The check is rule‑based and only fires on clear evidence, so it catches a small share of false positives, not all of them. “Confirmed” rows additionally appear in that question's own wrong_answers field.
